# Restaurants, Dining & Lounges

## 1. Azure Breeze Seafood Grill (Fine Dining)
- Concept: Mediterranean Coastal Cuisine & Fresh Sustainable Seafood
- Location: Cliffside Promenade, Level 1
- Operating Hours:
  - Breakfast: 6:30 AM - 10:30 AM (Monday to Friday); 6:30 AM - 11:00 AM (Saturday & Sunday)
  - Lunch: 12:00 PM - 3:00 PM Daily
  - Dinner: 5:30 PM - 10:30 PM Daily
- Dress Code: Smart Casual (Collared shirts for gentlemen, tailored trousers or elegant dresses; no athletic swimwear or flip-flops permitted during dinner).
- Reservations: Strongly recommended (can be booked via Concierge or OpenTable integration).
- Signature Dishes:
  - Pan-Seared Chilean Sea Bass with Truffle Sunchoke Puree ($58)
  - Whole Grilled Azure Bay Lobster with Herbs de Provence ($85)
  - Chilled Grand Seafood Plateau for two ($120)
  - Meyer Lemon Tart with Rosemary Infused Crème Fraîche ($18)
- Dietary Options: Comprehensive gluten-free and dairy-free menus; Halal-friendly preparations upon 24-hour notice.

## 2. Le Jardin French Bistro (Upscale Casual)
- Concept: Modern French Provençal cuisine in an airy courtyard setting
- Location: Botanical Courtyard Gardens, Ground Level
- Operating Hours:
  - Lunch: 11:30 AM - 2:30 PM Daily
  - Dinner: 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM Daily
  - Sunday Provençal Brunch: 10:30 AM - 2:30 PM (Sundays only, live acoustic jazz)
- Dress Code: Resort Elegant (smart casual denim permitted).
- Signature Dishes:
  - Duck Breast Magret with Cherry Port Reduction ($46)
  - Wild Forest Mushroom Risotto with 24-Month Aged Parmesan (Vegetarian, $36)
  - Traditional Bouillabaisse with Saffron Rouille ($52)
  - Soufflé au Grand Marnier ($20)
- Dietary Options: Vegetarian selections, vegan preparations available upon request.

## 3. Sunset Cliffside Lounge & Raw Bar
- Concept: Artisanal tapas, fresh sashimi, raw bar, and craft mixology
- Location: Infinity Terrace, Level 3
- Operating Hours: 4:00 PM - Midnight Daily. Happy Hour with live DJ from 4:30 PM - 6:00 PM.
- Dress Code: Resort Casual.
- Reservations: Walk-ins welcomed; reservations accepted for VIP fire pit banquettes.
- Signature Cocktails:
  - The Azure Sunset: Botanical gin, passionfruit, elderflower liqueur, butterfly pea blossom ($22)
  - Smoked Mezcalita: Artisanal mezcal, agave, blood orange, smoked sea salt rim ($24)
- Small Plates: Bluefin tuna tartare, Japanese A5 Wagyu beef sliders, seasonal oysters on the half-shell.

## 4. In-Room Dining (24/7 Room Service)
- Availability: Available 24 hours daily to all guest rooms, suites, and villas.
- Menu Highlights:
  - All-Day Breakfast: Organic acai bowls, Eggs Benedict on brioche, buttermilk blueberry pancakes.
  - Comfort Classics: Grand Azure Wagyu Burger ($34), Artisan Wood-Fired Margherita Pizza ($28), Club Sandwich with applewood smoked bacon ($26).
  - Late Night Menu (11:00 PM - 6:00 AM): Gourmet burgers, pizzas, artisanal charcuterie boards, warm chocolate chip cookies with organic milk.
- Delivery Fee: $6.00 tray delivery service fee plus 18% standard gratuity added to folio.
