# The Azure Spa & Holistic Wellness Center

## Overview & Facilities
The Azure Spa spans 25,000 square feet of tranquil sanctuary dedicated to restorative therapies, holistic skin wellness, and hydrotherapy.
- Operating Hours: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM Daily.
- Facilities Include:
  - Heated Seawater Vitality Pool (98°F)
  - Himalayan Salt Inhalation Chamber
  - Eucalyptus Herbal Steam Room & Finnish Dry Cedar Sauna
  - Ice Plunge Waterfall (52°F)
  - Ocean-view relaxation lounge with herbal tea and dried fruit elixir bar.

## Spa Booking & Etiquette Guidelines
- Advance Notice: Treatments should be booked at least 4 hours in advance (24 hours recommended for weekend slots).
- Arrival Time: Guests are requested to arrive 30 minutes prior to appointment time to enjoy thermal amenities.
- Cancellation Policy: Cancellations within 6 hours of scheduled treatment time incur a 50% cancellation fee. No-shows incur 100% fee.
- Minimum Age: Guests must be 16 years of age or older to access spa thermal facilities and receive body treatments.
