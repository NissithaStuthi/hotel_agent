# Hotel Facilities, Recreation & Guest Amenities

## 1. Multi-Tier Cliffside Infinity Pools
- Location: Levels 2 and 3 Cliff Promenade
- Hours: 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM Daily.
- Atmosphere & Rules:
  - Water temperature heated to 82°F (28°C) year-round.
  - Quiet Adults-Only Hours (ages 18+): 8:00 PM - 10:00 PM nightly.
  - Complimentary amenities: Chilled SPF mist, eucalyptus cold towels, iced fruit skewers delivered hourly by pool attendants.
  - Private Luxury Cabanas: $150 half-day / $280 full-day (includes fresh exotic fruit platter, stocked mini-fridge with sparkling water, dedicated cabana host).

## 2. 24/7 Panoramic Fitness Pavilion
- Location: North Wing, Level 2
- Hours: Open 24 hours daily with valid guest room keycard.
- Equipment:
  - Technogym Artis cardio equipment with interactive virtual training screens
  - Peloton commercial bikes (Peloton All-Access included)
  - Free weights up to 100 lbs, Olympic kettlebells, and TRX suspension bays.
- Classes & Activities:
  - Complimentary Sunrise Ocean Yoga on the Cliff Lawn daily at 7:30 AM (mats provided).
  - Private personal training sessions ($120/hr) bookable via Concierge.

## 3. Private Azure Beach Club & Marina
- Location: Secluded cove accessed via the scenic panoramic cliff glass elevator.
- Hours: 8:00 AM - 7:00 PM Daily.
- Water Sports:
  - Complimentary paddleboards, sea kayaks, and snorkeling gear for in-house guests.
  - Motorized water sports (jet skis, seabob underwater scooters) available with certified instructors.
  - Marina accommodates private guest yachts up to 150 feet with dockage reservations.

## 4. Valet Parking & Electric Vehicle Charging
- Location: Main Porte-Cochère, Level 1
- Overnight Valet Parking: $45 per night with 24-hour unlimited in-and-out privileges.
- Day Visitors: Complimentary valet with $75 minimum validation at restaurants or spa.
- EV Charging: 8 Tesla Superchargers and 6 universal Level-2 charging stations; charging is complimentary for registered resort guests.

## 5. Little Mariners Kids Club
- Location: Garden Pavilion, Ground Level
- Hours: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM Daily.
- Ages: 4 to 12 years (complimentary supervised activities, marine biology workshops, craft sessions).
- Babysitting: Private in-room certified babysitting service available at $35/hour (requires 12-hour advance booking through the concierge).
