# Hotel Policies, Terms & Guest Conduct Regulations

## 1. Check-In & Check-Out Policy
- Standard Check-In: 3:00 PM local time.
- Standard Check-Out: 11:00 AM local time.
- Early Check-In:
  - Subject to room availability on the day of arrival.
  - Complimentary after 12:00 PM for Executive Suite and Villa guests.
  - Guaranteed early arrival before 10:00 AM requires a half-night room charge.
- Late Check-Out:
  - Complimentary up to 1:00 PM upon request, subject to availability.
  - Late departure until 4:00 PM: $100 flat fee.
  - Departures after 4:00 PM: Full night room rate.
- Security Deposit & Authorization: A credit card pre-authorization of room rate + $150 per night incidental hold is taken at check-in. Remaining funds release within 3-5 business days after departure.

## 2. Cancellation & Modification Policy
- Standard Rooms & Suites: Cancellations or modifications must be received by 3:00 PM local time at least 48 hours prior to arrival to avoid penalty. Cancellations within 48 hours forfeit the first night's room rate and taxes.
- Cliffside Villas & Penthouse: Cancellations require at least 7 days advance notice prior to arrival date. Late cancellations forfeit 50% of the total reserved stay value.
- Non-Refundable Promotional Bookings: Fully non-refundable and non-changeable once confirmed.

## 3. Pet Policy (Canine Concierge Program)
- Pet-Friendly Rooms: Dogs up to 45 lbs (20 kg) are welcomed exclusively in Deluxe Double Queen Garden Terrace rooms and Cliffside Ocean Villas.
- Maximum: 2 dogs per accommodation.
- Sanitization Fee: $150 non-refundable one-time fee per stay.
- Pet Amenities Provided: Plush orthopedic dog bed, ceramic bowls, gourmet dog treats, walking maps.
- Restrictions: Dogs must be leashed in all public corridors and resort grounds. Not allowed in pool waters, spa treatment rooms, or inside closed restaurant dining rooms.
- Certified Service Animals: Fully exempt from all fees and size restrictions under ADA compliance.

## 4. 100% Smoke-Free Property Policy
- The entire resort is strictly non-smoking (this includes all guest rooms, suites, villas, private balconies, patios, and pool decks).
- Prohibited Substances: Traditional cigarettes, cigars, electronic cigarettes, vaping pens, and cannabis products.
- Designated Smoking Area: The only permitted smoking area is the outdoor North Garden Gazebo.
- Penalty: A mandatory deep cleaning, ozone deodorizing, and restoration fee of $500 will be charged to the guest's folio upon detection of smoking in non-designated zones.

## 5. Quiet Hours & Privacy Policy
- Quiet Hours: Observed between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM nightly.
- Excessive noise, high-volume music, and disruptive gatherings in rooms or balconies are prohibited.
- Drone Policy: Recreational drone flights are strictly prohibited across all resort grounds and airspace to protect guest privacy.
