# Local Attractions, Sightseeing & Transportation Services

## 1. Top Local Attractions & Concierge Recommendations
- **Emerald Cove Sea Caves & Kayak Sanctuary**:
  - Distance: 3.2 km (10-minute drive).
  - Highlights: Secluded crystalline grottos with bioluminescent waters. Best visited at morning high tide between 9:00 AM and 11:30 AM.
  - Concierge Service: The resort provides complimentary sea kayaks and packed gourmet picnic hampers.

- **Historic Old Town Citadel & Artisan Market**:
  - Distance: 7.5 km (18-minute drive or scenic ferry).
  - Highlights: 17th-century cobblestone fortress, Mediterranean perfumeries, artisan leather boutiques, open-air olive oil tastings.
  - Concierge Tip: Best in the late afternoon before golden hour sunset.

- **Monte Azure Cliffside Botanical Gardens**:
  - Distance: 5.0 km (12-minute drive).
  - Highlights: 15 acres of exotic cacti and subtropical flora clinging to dramatic sea cliffs.

- **Azure Marina Promenade**:
  - Distance: 1.5 km (5-minute drive / 15-minute coastal boardwalk walk).
  - Highlights: High-end shopping (Chanel, Rolex, Loro Piana), waterfront champagne bars, superyacht marina.
